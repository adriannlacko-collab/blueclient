BlueClient · flame mark concepts

A — Rising Ember

Leaning flame whose base curls into an open crescent — the closest match to the feather mark.

B — Starcore

Upright flame with its belly hollowed into a star-filled orb. Boldest silhouette; strongest at small sizes.

C — Moon Cradle

Solid flame rising out of a crescent moon, with twinkles around it. Simplest shapes, most "campfire".
